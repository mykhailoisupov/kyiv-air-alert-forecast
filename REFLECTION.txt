What went wrong? My first model, logistic regression on all history, looked strong (+8% to +20% Brier skill)
only because my reference forecast was weak: a flat 28-day rate beat it. Since the September alert reform that
model forecast too low and lost to a simple rule at 6 hours. I also wrote that boosted trees "failed outright";
a review showed most of that was my calibration wrapper.

How did I adjust? I scored everything against the strongest simple forecast, split results by period, trained
on the last 16 weeks only, and re-ran the trees without the wrapper.

Why is the final version better? Since the reform it beats the best simple forecast by 10-12% at 3-6 hours.
Over the full eight weeks it is not better, and the README says so.
