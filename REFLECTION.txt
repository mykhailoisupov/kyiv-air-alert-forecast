What went wrong? My first model, logistic regression on all history, looked strong (+8% to +20% Brier skill)
only because my reference forecast was weak; a flat 28-day rate beat it. Since the September reform it
forecast too low. I also blamed boosted trees for a failure caused mostly by my own calibration wrapper.

How did I adjust? I scored everything against the strongest simple forecast, split results by period,
trained on 16 recent weeks, and re-ran the trees without the wrapper.

Why is the final version better? Since the reform it beats the best simple forecast by 10-12% at 3-6 hours.
Over all eight weeks it is not better than the first attempt, and I say so.
