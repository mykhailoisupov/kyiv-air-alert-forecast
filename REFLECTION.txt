What went wrong? My first model, logistic regression on all history, looked strong (+8% to +20% Brier skill) only because my reference forecast was weak: a flat 28-day rate beat it. Since the September alert reform that model forecast too low and lost to a simple rule at 6 hours. Boosted trees failed outright.

How did I adjust? I scored everything against the strongest simple forecast, split results by period, and trained on the last 16 weeks only.

Why is the final version better? Since the reform it beats the best simple forecast by 10-12% at 3-6 hours. Over the full eight weeks it is not better, and the README says so.
