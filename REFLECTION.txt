What went wrong? My first model, a logistic regression trained on all history, looked strong (+8% to +20% Brier skill) only because my reference, an hour-of-day climatology, was weak. A flat 28-day rate and a simple recent-onset rule beat that reference. Since the September alert reform the first model fell behind the simple rule at 6 hours and forecast too low. Boosted trees failed outright.

How did I adjust? I scored every forecast against the strongest simple one, split results by period, and trained on the last 16 weeks only.

Why is the final version better? Since the reform it beats the best simple forecast by 10-12% at 3-6 hours and its average forecast is close to what happened. Over the full eight weeks it is not better than the first attempt, and the README says so.
